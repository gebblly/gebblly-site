Account Deletion Request – Gebblly

Gebblly allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data. This page is provided for users of the Gebblly Customer App, Restaurant App, and Driver App.

To request deletion of your Gebblly account, please send an email to:
gebblly@gmail.com

Information to include in your request

Please include the following details so we can identify and verify your account:

  • Full name
  • Phone number or email address used in the app
  • User type: Customer, Restaurant, or Driver
  • Reason for deletion, optional

What happens after your request

After receiving your request, Gebblly will verify account ownership and process the deletion request within a reasonable period.

Data that may be deleted

Data that may be deleted includes:

  • Account profile information
  • Contact details
  • Saved addresses
  • App usage data linked to the account
  • Order-related account data, where legally permitted

Data that may be retained

Some data may be retained where required for legal, tax, security, fraud prevention, payment, dispute resolution, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Gebblly handles personal data, please review our Privacy Policy.
